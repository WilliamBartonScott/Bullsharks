About the Club

Freedivers,
for Freedivers

The Brisbane Bullsharks is a freediving club that welcomes both freedivers and spearfishers. Our training program is designed to enhance freediving skills across all experience levels — from beginners to competition level.

We exist to create a safe training environment where divers can thoroughly learn and practice apnea and the technique of the sport — to foster continual advancement of knowledge, technique, and physical ability.

How to Train
With Us

01

Join the AFA

All participants must be members of the Australian Freediving Association. The AFA provides insurance required for pool training and promotes safe practices across the sport.

australianfreediving.org/membership →

  • Have-A-Go: Valid for 2 weeks — great for your first induction
  • Bullsharks Member: For those training exclusively with us
  • Dual Member: Train with both Bullsharks and BFC
02

Complete the Safety Induction

Before joining regular training, all new members complete a Bullsharks Safety Induction. This is held on the first Tuesday of each month at The Valley Pool at 6:00 PM.

Register your AFA membership before attending — it must be confirmed in advance.

03

Gear Up & Dive In

You don't need much to get started. Basic gear for your first session:

  • Mask & snorkel
  • Fins (freediving fins preferred, not essential)
  • Weights for neutral buoyancy
  • Wetsuit (optional but recommended)
